# Project maintenance notes

Before changing advisor decisions, retrieval, tools, or evaluations, read
`docs_agents/README.md` and its current-state and experiment notes. They record
user preferences, adopted changes, failed experiments, and evaluation limits.

Keep persistent engineering notes in `docs_agents`; update existing notes instead
of adding another dated report to `docs` after every experiment. User-facing
`docs/run-stats.md` and `docs/SLS2MCP_api.md` must be preserved. New user instructions
take precedence over these notes.
